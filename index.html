About

Nebula.Now

A require-script organizer only. It stores and copies text; it does not run Roblox modules. Third-party modules can be changed, deleted or made private by their owners, so verify modules before using them in a place you control.
Does Nebula.Now execute scripts?

No. This page is a front-end organizer and copier. It does not itself execute Roblox modules.

Where are accounts and saved items stored?

The current build stores account/session information and saved items in the local browser. They are not synced between devices.

Why can an item stop working?

A third-party module can be edited, removed, restricted or made private by its owner. The entry in this library can remain visible even when the external module changes.

What does the Executors section show?

It presents compatibility/status information from the public WEAO tracker when the feed is available. The page does not mirror direct exploit download or purchase links.
